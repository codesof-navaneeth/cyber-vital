Category BMI
Underweight < 18.5
Healthy 18.5 – 24.9
Overweight 25.0 – 29.9
Obesity 30.0+
BMI is a screening measure based on height and weight. It does not directly measure body fat or distinguish muscle from fat, so it should not be treated as a complete picture of health.
This calculator is for general informational use. BMI categories can be interpreted differently for children, teenagers, pregnancy and some athletes. It is not a diagnosis.
